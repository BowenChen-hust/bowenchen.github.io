#### Short Bio
I’m currently a PhD student in the joint BME program at New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT) and Rutgers University.

At the moment, I’m mainly interested in using AI models for cancer prediction and for understanding the brain and neural systems. My background is in optical engineering and biomedical imaging, and I’m particularly interested in combining imaging technologies with machine learning to better understand disease progression and brain function.

In the long term, I hope to develop AI and imaging methods that can be translated into real clinical and biomedical applications.
